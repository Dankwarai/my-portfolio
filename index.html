About

Developer by passion, technician by trade

The Developer

I build backend systems and full-stack web applications using PHP, JavaScript, TypeScript and React. My projects range from multi-section school management platforms to QR-based restaurant ordering systems with Paystack payments and a property marketplace for Nigeria.

Being hands-on at client sites has taught me how the software I write is actually used in the real world.
